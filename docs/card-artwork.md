# Card artwork

Updates, Courses and Curricula share a code-driven artwork generator. It renders SVG lines and shapes without an AI service, image API, or per-card raster files. The generated artwork is part of the card interface, not an Open Graph sharing image.

## Authoring

In the Update or Course editor, or in the Curriculum manager, open **Card artwork**. Enter a short title of at most 40 displayed characters for generated art. It can differ from the full title, which remains ordinary card text below the art with the description. **Shuffle artwork** draws a random seed in the browser and avoids the 25 most recently seen compositions in that editing session. Ten motif families have three compositions each: rings, flowing contours, nested frames, diagonals, arcs, waves, peaks and steps, bubbles and constellations, wide bands, and radiating beams. Three seeded parameters add structural variation: rhythm couples count and spacing; proportion changes dimensions or curvature; placement moves the focal area. Each family constrains these parameters to keep its shapes related. Linework uses a restrained hierarchy of weights and opacity; broad color shapes have no border strokes. Nested shapes share consistent geometry, and the artwork fades toward the short title. Light and dark treatment adds further variety. No AI tokens or server request are used. The choice stays a local preview until the item is saved. Draft changes to Updates and Courses do not change their published cards until they are published.

In an installed Fieldbook, an administrator or contributor may upload a JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF image within the installation's application and storage limits. Transfers show progress; large images use chunked uploads with bounded retries. A failed transfer or verification leaves the existing artwork intact and can be retried by choosing the file again. A custom image replaces only the art area. It uses the existing private media upload path and must be ready before the item can be saved. The browser-local demo saves generated artwork; it does not upload files. Switching back to Generated restores the short title and selected design. Removing or replacing a card image does not delete older media files. Existing Course covers remain valid card images and video posters; a later generated card choice does not remove that poster.

## Installation palette

**Administration → Organization settings → Identity → Card artwork** offers **Follow installation accent** (the default), eight presets, and **Custom three colors**. Custom colors are Primary / canvas, Accent 1 / lines, and Accent 2 / highlights, each a six-digit hex color. Follow accent derives all three colors from the saved installation accent. Changing the palette recolors generated art across the installation while preserving each card's composition. Uploaded images retain their own colors. The generator derives light and dark backgrounds from the palette and places a soft contrast fade behind short titles.

## Compatibility

Each generated item stores a seed and generator version. The saved seed makes the selected artwork repeatable on every view. Saved versions 1 through 5 continue to render with their original geometry; a new Shuffle selects version 6 artwork. Items without artwork settings keep the version 2 design based on their ID and a shortened title, including new items until Shuffle is used. Editing the short title or switching between upload and generated modes preserves the saved version and seed. No data rewrite is needed. The server validates saved artwork and palette fields. No database migration is required because content, curricula, and settings already use JSON fields. The admin MCP continues to accept older payloads without artwork fields. Private image access follows the installation's existing authentication and publication rules.
